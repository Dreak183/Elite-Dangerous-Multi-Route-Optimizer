# Cowork brief — EDMRN

Role, rules and research order: `../../../cowork/INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Read that first.

## The project

EDMRN is a fork of *ED Multi Route Navigation* (NinurtaKalhu/Elite-Dangerous-Multi-Route-Optimizer,
AGPL-3.0), a Python/customtkinter desktop route planner for Elite Dangerous. The fork adds journal
location detection, saved routes, system bookmarks, and an exchange channel through which a wing
leader sends routes to a small group of friends.

## Research backlog

Transport, AGPL §13 and community sharing formats are researched: `../../../knowledge/group-sync-transport.md`
and `../../../knowledge/ed-data-sharing.md`. Open:

1. **Discord Developer Policy on machine-to-machine data.** Whether a bot or webhook may carry
   automated exploration posts generated by a desktop tool (the policy page returned 403 to
   automated fetches).
2. **Exploration journal events for "valuable finds".** Field shapes and value sets, verified
   against real journals: `FSSDiscoveryScan`, `FSSAllBodiesFound`, `Scan` (`PlanetClass`,
   `Landable`, `TerraformState`, `WasDiscovered`, `WasMapped`), `FSSBodySignals` and
   `SAASignalsFound` signal types (Biological, Geological, Thargoid, Guardian, Human, Other),
   `ScanOrganic`, `CodexEntry`; and how notable stellar phenomena appear in the journal
   (`FSSSignalDiscovered` signal names/types or `CodexEntry` categories).
3. **Hull health and loadout in the journal.** Which events carry hull health between `Loadout`
   events (`HullDamage` thresholds, repairs, `Status.json`), and which carry fuel capacity.
